## Generating forms through rules

This collection uses Grasshopper for Rhino and related plugins for parametric modelling. Changing rules and parameters explores branches, openings and continuous surfaces that are difficult to construct directly through conventional modelling.

![Desktop container model with a branching growth structure](/images/projects/parametric-objects/branch-form.webp)

## Different expressions of desktop storage

Open branching structures and perforated geometries are explored through pen holders and desktop storage. Everyday storage functions become a setting for experiments in form.

![Physical branching pen holder](/images/projects/parametric-objects/printed-branch.webp)
![Physical desktop storage object with geometric openings](/images/projects/parametric-objects/printed-holder.webp)

## Continuous surfaces and openings

The exploration extends from practical containers to sculptural objects, using continuous surfaces and repeated openings to create a visual rhythm in complex geometry.

![Sculptural object formed from continuous surfaces and repeated openings](/images/projects/parametric-objects/printed-knot.webp)
